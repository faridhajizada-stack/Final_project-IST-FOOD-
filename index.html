About Us

IST FOOD was founded by two friends who shared a passion for great food and a dream of creating something special together. Our journey began in 2023 in Králův Dvůr, where we opened our first small place focused mainly on delicious, freshly prepared kebabs. From the very beginning, our goal was simple — serve quality food, use fresh ingredients, and give every customer a great experience.

Thanks to the amazing support from our customers, IST FOOD quickly became a local favorite. By 2025, we expanded and opened two more branches, introducing a new concept that combines pizza, burgers, and kebabs in one place. This concept allowed us to offer a wider variety of flavors while still keeping the quality and authenticity that our guests love.

Today, we are proud to maintain an average rating of 4.7 stars across all our branches, reflecting the trust and satisfaction of our customers. In February 2026, we opened our newest branch in Hostivice, closer to Prague, bringing our concept even closer to the capital.

This location follows our popular pizza, burger, and kebab concept, while also offering beer, cocktails, and delicious desserts for an even better dining experience.

At IST FOOD, we believe in doing things our own way — Not the same as others. Every meal we serve represents our passion, dedication, and love for great food shared with great people.
